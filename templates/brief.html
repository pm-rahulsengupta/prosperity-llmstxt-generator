How these answers are used

The URL patterns are enforced in code, not asked of a model. Declaring a pattern valuable stops it being excluded on traffic alone — it is held for your review instead — but it does not force it into the file, because one broad pattern would otherwise admit an entire faceted URL space. Declaring a pattern low value is the mirror: it is never included wholesale, but a page inside it that earns real clicks is still kept.

Embargoed patterns are excluded outright and are never sent to a model. The free-text answers are given to the planning and description stages as context and have no automatic effect on what is included.
