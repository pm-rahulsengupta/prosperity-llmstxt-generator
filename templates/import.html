In Screaming Frog: Internal tab, filter All, then Export. The columns this needs are Address, Status Code, Content Type, Indexability, Title, Meta Description, H1, Word Count and the inlink counts — all of which that export carries by default.

What happens to it

Only indexable HTML pages that answered 200 are kept — Screaming Frog's own verdict is trusted, since it saw the response and we did not.

The crawl plan is skipped. That review step exists to decide what to fetch before paying to fetch it. You have already crawled, and already chosen what to export, so there is nothing left to decide. Triage, summarising and assembly run as normal.

Embargo patterns from the brief still apply. A row matching one is discarded and never stored, exactly as a crawl would refuse to fetch it — an upload must not be a way around a rule about what may be kept.
